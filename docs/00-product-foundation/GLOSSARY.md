# Glossary

- **Tenant:** A subscribing customer organization whose data is isolated from other customers.
- **Legal entity:** A registered business entity that owns transactions and statutory accounts.
- **Branch:** A retail location belonging to a legal entity.
- **Register:** A POS workstation or logical checkout terminal.
- **IMEI:** International Mobile Equipment Identity; a device identifier. Validate format/check digit where applicable, but do not assume every device has one or only one identifier.
- **Serialized item:** An individually tracked physical asset with serial/IMEI identity.
- **Bulk item:** Quantity-tracked stock such as cases, chargers and screen protectors.
- **Stock ledger:** Append-only record of inventory movements.
- **Reservation:** A time-bounded allocation of stock to a transaction or workflow.
- **Trade-in:** Acquisition of a customer's used device, potentially as purchase, store credit or part payment.
- **Consignment:** Stock physically held by the retailer but owned by a supplier until contractually triggered.
- **Subledger:** Detailed AP, AR, inventory or fixed-asset records that reconcile to general-ledger control accounts.
- **Offline outbox:** Durable local queue of commands/events awaiting server acknowledgement.
- **Idempotency key:** Unique request identifier that prevents duplicate effects on retries.
- **Jurisdiction pack:** Reviewed country/region configuration and rules for tax, documents, numbering and statutory reporting.
