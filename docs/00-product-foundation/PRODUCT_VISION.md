# Product Vision

## Vision statement
Give independent smartphone retailers the operational control and financial clarity of a mature retail chain without the cost or complexity traditionally associated with enterprise ERP software.

## Problem
Mobile retailers manage high-value devices with unique IMEIs, fast-changing costs, accessories, repairs, trade-ins, returns and cash across staff and branches. Generic POS products often treat stock as interchangeable quantities and separate repairs or buybacks from inventory and accounting. This creates shrinkage, inconsistent valuations, weak margins and unreliable records.

## Product promise
One connected system from supplier purchase to device sale, repair, return, accounting entry and customer aftercare—with a fast counter experience and continuity during internet outages.

## Differentiators
- Every serialized device has a traceable identity and lifecycle.
- Repairs consume identifiable parts and produce warranty obligations.
- Trade-ins become controlled acquisitions and inventory assets.
- POS, stock and accounting are connected by auditable events.
- Offline operation is designed around explicit conflict and reconciliation rules.
- Global-ready foundations with localized statutory behaviour.

## Success measures
Measure activation time, first sale completion, weekly active stores, checkout duration, stock variance, percentage of serialized units with complete histories, repair turnaround, trade-in conversion, gross margin accuracy, reconciliation completion, sync conflict rate, retention and support burden.

Do not optimize only for feature count. A small shop should understand the product quickly and complete daily work without specialist training.
