# Flutter Staff App Specification

Platforms: iOS and Android phones/tablets, with optional rugged scanner terminal support through tested device adapters.

Primary workflows:
- Login/device registration and assigned branch selection.
- Camera/barcode/IMEI scanning and stock lookup.
- Trade-in grading, photos, signatures and offer presentation.
- Repair intake, damage photos/annotations, technician updates and parts requests.
- Cycle counts and transfer dispatch/receipt.
- Customer lookup within permission.
- Offline task capture and sync status.

UX: one-handed thumb-zone actions, large scan/capture controls, fast camera viewfinder, clear step progress and accessible contrast. Offline storage must be encrypted, minimized and synchronized with the same idempotency/conflict model as desktop.
