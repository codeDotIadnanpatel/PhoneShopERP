# Audit, Records and Retention

Audit high-impact actions: authentication events, role changes, tenant configuration, price overrides, sales void/refund, IMEI lifecycle changes, stock adjustments, trade-in acceptance, repair part usage, journal posting, period close, exports, sensitive evidence access and support impersonation/access.

Audit records include actor, tenant/entity/branch, action, target, timestamp, correlation ID, reason and before/after summary where safe. Avoid logging passwords, tokens, payment card data, device passcodes or unnecessary identity details.

Retention is jurisdiction- and record-type-specific. Provide configurable schedules, legal holds, export, anonymization and deletion workflows. Financial and statutory records may need retention after customer account closure. Publish a privacy notice and data processing terms.
