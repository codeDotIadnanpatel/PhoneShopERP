# Bulk Inventory, Parts and Stock Control

Support quantity-based accessories, spare parts, consumables and non-serialized products. Track on-hand, reserved, available, damaged, quarantined, in-transit and consignment quantities by stock location.

Features:
- Reorder points, min/max, safety stock and low-stock alerts.
- Multiple units of measure and conversion rules where needed.
- Batch/lot and expiry tracking for applicable products.
- Stock counts, cycle counts, blind counts and variance approval.
- Adjustments with reason codes and accounting impact.
- Transfers with dispatch and receipt steps.
- Supplier returns and customer returns.
- Kits/bundles with component deduction rules.
- Negative stock policy configurable by tenant/product and restricted by role.

Never derive stock solely from a mutable quantity field. Maintain a stock movement ledger and materialized balance projections that can be reconciled.
