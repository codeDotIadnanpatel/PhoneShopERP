# Product Catalogue and Pricing

Support brands, categories, product families, models, variants, attributes, storage, colour, condition, compatibility, suppliers, SKU, multiple barcodes, images, descriptions and active/inactive lifecycle.

Product tracking modes:
- Serialized: each physical unit is a distinct asset (phones, tablets, watches and selected parts).
- Quantity-based: stock is counted in units or defined units of measure (cases, chargers, screen protectors, cables and consumables).
- Service/non-stock: labour, diagnostics, setup, delivery and fees.
- Bundle/kit: a commercial offer composed of products/services with explicit component and stock deduction rules.

Pricing:
- Multiple price lists by branch/channel/customer group/currency.
- Cost, retail, promotional and minimum-margin guardrails.
- Tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive price display according to jurisdiction.
- Bundle discounts and configurable discount approval.
- Effective dates, price history and authorized overrides.
- Avoid treating a product's current average cost as the cost of every serialized unit.

Acceptance: catalogue changes are audited; inactive products remain available in historical documents; barcode uniqueness is enforced within the tenant policy.
