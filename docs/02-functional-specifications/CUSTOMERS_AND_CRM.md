# Customers and CRM

Maintain customer identity and contact details, preferred language, communication consent, purchase history, repair history, trade-in history, warranties, store credit, loyalty balance and notes with access controls.

Support individual and business customers, duplicate detection, merge with audit, customer groups, account terms where enabled, marketing preferences and data subject requests. Collect only necessary personal data. Do not expose customer records across tenants. Consent must be purpose-specific, timestamped and revocable.
