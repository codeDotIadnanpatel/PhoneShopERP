# Purchasing and Suppliers

Support supplier master records, contacts, currencies, payment terms, tax details, bank/payment references and supplier performance.

Workflow:
1. Create purchase requisition or purchase order.
2. Approve according to amount/category policy.
3. Send/export order to supplier.
4. Receive full or partial shipment; scan IMEIs and count bulk quantities.
5. Record discrepancies, damaged goods, backorders and supplier returns.
6. Capture supplier invoice and match against order/receipt.
7. Allocate freight, insurance, customs and other landed costs according to a documented allocation method.
8. Post inventory and AP accounting entries.
9. Pay supplier through cash/bank/payment allocation and reconcile.

Support multiple currencies, exchange rates, supplier credit notes, deposits, consignment agreements and vendor-managed stock. Purchase cost must be traceable to each serialized unit or allocated bulk receipt.
