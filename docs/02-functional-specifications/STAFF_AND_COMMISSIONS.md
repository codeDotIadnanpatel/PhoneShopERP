# Staff, Incentives and Commissions

Maintain staff profiles, branch assignments, active status, sales attribution, repair assignment, commission plans, spiffs, targets and performance history. Do not position this module as a complete payroll engine unless separately scoped.

Commission rules may be based on net sales, gross margin, category, service labour, trade-in conversion or fixed bonuses. Exclude refunded/voided transactions or claw back according to transparent policy. Support caps, tiers, shared sales and manager approval. Commission accruals must map to accounting liability/expense accounts and be traceable to source transactions.
