# Trade-In, Buyback and Used Device Acquisition

## Intake
Identify customer and device, capture IMEI/serial, model, storage, colour, ownership declaration and acquisition purpose. Collect identity evidence only where legally required; configure jurisdiction-specific fields, retention and access.

## Grading wizard
Guided checklist covers screen, housing, frame, battery health, camera, biometric functions, buttons, speakers, microphones, charging port, connectivity and other configurable tests. Capture notes, photos and customer acknowledgement. Record tester and time.

## Valuation
Support store-defined grading matrices, cost/margin floors, manual authorized offers and optional market-price provider integrations. Show quote validity, assumptions and approval. External market values are estimates, not guaranteed prices.

## Acceptance and accounting
Customer accepts terms; capture required signature/consent; record payout method or trade-in credit. Create acquisition document, supplier/customer settlement as appropriate, inventory asset with used/refurbished condition and linked IMEI history. Route to available stock, quarantine or repair/refurbishment.

## Compliance
Country pack determines KYC/ID requirements, declarations, reporting and retention. Restrict evidence access and log every view/export. Do not assert universal police reporting requirements.
