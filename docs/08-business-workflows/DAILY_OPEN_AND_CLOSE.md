# Daily Opening and Closing

## Opening
1. Staff signs in and selects authorized branch/register.
2. App loads local data and displays connectivity/sync health.
3. Cashier starts shift and records opening float.
4. System checks unresolved sync conflicts, register status and required setup.
5. Cashier begins sales.

## Closing
1. Complete or park active carts.
2. Review pending transactions and sync queue.
3. Count cash and record other tender settlements.
4. Compare expected and counted totals; record variance reason.
5. Submit shift close for approval if policy requires.
6. Reconcile cash/card clearing and post required accounting events.
7. Manager reviews exceptions and closes branch day where configured.
