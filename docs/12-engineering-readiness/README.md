# Engineering Readiness & AI Development Framework

This directory adds the v1.4 engineering-readiness layer. It supplements, and does not replace, the product specification, accepted decisions, phase plans or v1.2 engineering design documents.

**Read first:** root `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, then root `AGENTS.md`, `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, the phase tracker and the relevant documents in this directory.

The documents distinguish:
- **Approved:** already established by the project decisions.
- **Required baseline:** recommended engineering control to adopt consistently.
- **Proposed / requires approval:** business policy or implementation detail not yet settled.
- **Illustrative:** example only, not a production contract.

Do not silently convert proposals or illustrative examples into approved product behaviour. Record material approvals in `DECISIONS.md` and update dependent specifications and tests.

For Phase 00 audit remediation and decision control, also read `../13-audit-remediation/README.md`.
